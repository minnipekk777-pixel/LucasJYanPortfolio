Self Driving Bot ← Click to view arduino build, parts & code!

A clever car that detects walls & self drives through any route!

  • Ultrasonic Sensor: Measures distance (between itself & incoming walls) by sending out ultrasonic/high frequency sound waves.
  • Motors & Chip: DC motors allow wheels to rotate cw/ccw, motor chip combines power wires & digital pin wires (motors can be controlled).
  • Signaling LED's & buzzer: LED's act as left/right turn signals, buzzer plays a note (indicating when the bot has reached a wall/dead end).
Electric Gumball Machine ← Click to view 3D interior, parts & code!

Insert a coin and out comes a gumball!

  • Coin Detection & Slide: LDR (light) sensor detects incoming coins down the track.
  • Motorized Paddle, Feeding Slide & Feedbowl : Rotating paddle pushes gumballs in circular motion within the cylindrical container.
  • Gumball Verification: Secondary LDR (light) sensor detects outgoing gumball -> motor stops -> ensures only 1 gumball comes out.
Lock Box ← Click to view 3D interior, parts & code!

Create and enter a passkey to lock up whatever you want.

  • Potentiometer, Button & LDR Sensor: Reads user input and door positioning.
  • DC Motor: Rotates a hook-like arm into a loop to lock and unlock the door.
  • 7-Segment Display & Status LEDs: Displays real time digits and lockbox status.
